The Team

We Make.

TalaTek Media Productions aspires to be the leading multimedia studio in Olongapo City, guided by the belief that creativity grows when shared. Our mission is to make stories, animations, and projects that reflect culture, community, and imagination. We are committed to nurturing local talent, building collaborations, and growing steadily while staying true to our roots. More than just producing content, we make opportunities, connections, and a space where artists can thrive.
