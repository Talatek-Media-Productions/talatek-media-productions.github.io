TalaTek Brand Identity

February 12, 2026

The TalaTek Media Productions identity pairs a star emblem with a high-contrast serif wordmark. A solid black square anchors the mark, an outlined star breaks past its edges, and a single golden-orange star at the centre carries the only colour in the design.

The lockup

TALATEK sits in large serif capitals with MEDIA PRODUCTIONS beneath it in wide letter-spaced caps, sharing the full width of the wordmark.

In application

The brand sheet shows the lockup repeated over the studio’s illustrated Olongapo scenes — the public market with its green roof, the town street with its white van, and the hillside of crosses — to show how the identity holds up over imagery.
