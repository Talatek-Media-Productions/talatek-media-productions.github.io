Messenger Theme Series

February 9, 2026

TalaTek designed a set of illustrated themes for its Messenger group chat. Each theme is a three-panel composition set around familiar Olongapo City landmarks — the city public market and its slogan walls, the street with its white van and the bird monument, and the hillside of white crosses.

The scenes are drawn as flat 2D illustration so the artwork stays legible at chat background size, and the TalaTek star-and-wordmark lockup sits on top so the group reads as branded at a glance.

The three themes

Theme 1 — three stacked panels: the salakot-wearing figure above the street scene, the two students on the green hill, and the student in the animal hat against the market wall.

Theme 2 — a tilted street scene, the three crosses on the hill, and the public market sign wall.

Theme 3 — a light-blue poster layout, the same three scenes presented as polaroid-style frames with the TalaTek characters placed around them.
