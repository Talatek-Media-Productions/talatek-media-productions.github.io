The TalaTek Media Productions identity pairs a star emblem with a high-contrast
serif wordmark. A solid black square anchors the mark, an outlined star breaks
past its edges, and a single golden-orange star at the centre carries the only
colour in the design.

## The lockup

TALATEK sits in large serif capitals with MEDIA PRODUCTIONS beneath it in wide
letter-spaced caps, sharing the full width of the wordmark.

![TalaTek Media Productions logo lockup](/assets/works/logo-lockup-sheet.png)

## In application

The brand sheet shows the lockup repeated over the studio's illustrated Olongapo
scenes — the public market with its green roof, the town street with its white
van, and the hillside of crosses — to show how the identity holds up over
imagery.

![TalaTek logo applied over illustrated Olongapo scenes](/assets/works/logo-brand-sheet.png)
