TalaTek designed a set of illustrated themes for its Messenger group chat. Each
theme is a three-panel composition set around familiar Olongapo City landmarks —
the city public market and its slogan walls, the street with its white van and
the bird monument, and the hillside of white crosses.

The scenes are drawn as flat 2D illustration so the artwork stays legible at chat
background size, and the TalaTek star-and-wordmark lockup sits on top so the
group reads as branded at a glance.

## The three themes

**Theme 1** — three stacked panels: the salakot-wearing figure above the street
scene, the two students on the green hill, and the student in the animal hat
against the market wall.

![Messenger theme 1](/assets/works/messenger-theme-1.png)

**Theme 2** — a tilted street scene, the three crosses on the hill, and the
public market sign wall.

![Messenger theme 2](/assets/works/messenger-theme-2.png)

**Theme 3** — a light-blue poster layout, the same three scenes presented as
polaroid-style frames with the TalaTek characters placed around them.
